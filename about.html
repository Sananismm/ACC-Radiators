Radiator engineering and anatomy

Four design variables decide how well a radiator sheds heat.

Core thickness

More depth means more coolant contact, limited by the space behind the grille.

Fin density

Fin density affects heat dissipation and airflow. Ask ACC for the specification of your selected radiator.

Tube pitch

Tube spacing sets coolant flow and how evenly the core sheds heat.

Header plate crimping

A tight crimp seals the tanks to the core and resists leaks under pressure and vibration.

Aluminum vs. copper-brass

PropertyAluminumCopper-Brass
Thermal efficiencyHigh, with dense brazed finsGood, slightly lower per unit volume
WeightLightHeavier
ConstructionPlastic tanks or welded aluminum; confirm the selected product's specificationCopper-brass core; confirm tank construction
RepairabilityLimited; usually replacedEasily solder-repaired in local workshops
Typical use caseHatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, 4x4sCommercial fleets, older vehicles
