Radiator engineering and anatomy

Four design variables decide how well a radiator sheds heat.

Core thickness

More depth means more coolant contact, limited by the space behind the grille.

Fin density

We use 14–16 fins per inch (FPI) to balance heat dissipation and airflow.

Tube pitch

Tube spacing sets coolant flow and how evenly the core sheds heat.

Header plate crimping

A tight crimp seals the tanks to the core and resists leaks under pressure and vibration.
