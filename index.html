Radiator technologies

Three core constructions cover everything from city hatchbacks to commercial fleets.

Plastic-Aluminum

Brazed aluminum, plastic tanks

The modern OE standard for hatchbacks and sedans: light, corrosion resistant and thermally efficient.

All-Aluminum

TIG-welded performance core

High-capacity cooling with no plastic end-tanks, made for 4x4s, SUVs and high ambient temperatures.

Copper-Brass

Traditional and repairable

Durable and easy to solder-repair in local workshops. Ideal for fleets and high-vibration use.
